Community Park Cleanup

Organized by Green Earth Foundation

About This Event

Join us for a meaningful morning of community service as we work together to restore and beautify Riverside Park. Volunteers will help collect litter, plant native species, remove invasive plants, and improve trail accessibility. All equipment and refreshments will be provided. No experience necessary — just bring your enthusiasm!

This event is perfect for individuals, families, and corporate groups looking to give back to the community. Together, we've cleaned over 2 tonnes of waste from this park since 2023!

What to Bring
  • Comfortable clothes you don't mind getting dirty
  • Closed-toe shoes
  • Water bottle
  • Sunscreen and hat (outdoor event)
