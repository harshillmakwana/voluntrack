Process

How VolunTrack Works

01

Register

Volunteers and organizations create accounts with detailed profiles and required information.

02

Get Approved

Admins review and approve registrations ensuring quality and authenticity of participants.

03

Create Events

Organizations publish events with tasks, required skills, timelines, and location details.

Track Progress

Monitor task completion, event status, and volunteer performance in real time.
